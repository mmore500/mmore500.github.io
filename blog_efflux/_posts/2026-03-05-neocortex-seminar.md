abstract: |
  The Cerebras Wafer-Scale Engine (WSE), with its 880,000 processors, opens new opportunities for digital experiments in evolutionary biology, enabling exploration of cross-scale phenomena such as many-species eco-evolutionary dynamics and evolutionary transitions in individuality.
  A central challenge in realizing this potential is tracking simulation dynamics that unfold across a vast, highly-distributed fabric of memory-constrained processors.
  This seminar presents sampling- and estimation-based strategies for collecting meaningful data from such simulations, and reviews recent work migrating experiment and data management pipelines for general-purpose, SDK-based wafer-scale computing to the Cerebras Wafer-Scale Cloud.
