---
layout: recipe
title: "Walnut Biscotti"
date: 2026-08-10
recipe:
  servings: about 20 biscotti
  prep: 20 min.
  cook: 40 min.
  preheat: 350 F
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    * 2 c. all-purpose flour
    * 1/2 c. granulated sugar
    * 1 tsp. baking powder
    * 1/4 tsp. salt
    * 1 c. chopped walnuts
    * 4 eggs
    * 1 tsp. vanilla extract
    * 1 tsp. almond extract

  directions_markdown: |-
    1. preheat oven to 350 F and line a large baking sheet with parchment
    2. whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt; stir in walnuts
    3. in a separate bowl, whisk eggs, vanilla extract, and almond extract until blended
    4. pour wet mixture into dry ingredients and stir until a slightly sticky dough forms, kneading by hand at the end if needed
    5. divide dough in half; shape each half into an 8-inch by 2-inch log on the baking sheet, spaced a few inches apart
    6. bake 22-25 minutes, until firm to the touch and lightly golden
    7. remove from oven (leave oven on) and let logs cool on the pan for exactly 10 minutes
    8. using a serrated knife, cut logs on a slight diagonal into 1/2-inch thick slices
    9. place slices cut-side down on the baking sheet; bake 6-7 minutes, flip, then bake another 6-7 minutes until dry and golden
    10. cool on a wire rack; biscotti continue to crisp as they cool

  notes_markdown: |-
    made without the usual oil for a leaner, less rich dough
    don't skip the 10-minute rest before slicing: too hot and the logs crumble, too long and they're hard to cut cleanly

---
